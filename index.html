Questions & Answers

Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about creating your YUÉLÍN account, redeeming product codes, earning points, and claiming rewards.

What is the YUÉLÍN App?

YUÉLÍN is a digital rewards platform where customers can register for an account, redeem eligible codes from purchased YUÉLÍN products, collect points, complete activities, unlock achievements, invite friends, and exchange accumulated points for available rewards. It gives you another way to enjoy your YUÉLÍN experience beyond the products themselves. The platform is powered by XMARKET.

Is creating a YUÉLÍN account free?

Yes. Creating a standard YUÉLÍN account is free. Simply visit the YUÉLÍN website, open the registration page, provide the required information, create your login credentials, and complete the registration process. You can then log in to explore the available features, redeem eligible product codes, collect points, and discover rewards. Optional services, such as VIP membership, may have separate fees.

How do I create my YUÉLÍN account?

Visit the YUÉLÍN registration page, enter the required information, choose a username, provide an active email address, and create a secure password. Complete any verification steps displayed on the website and submit your registration. Once your account has been successfully created, log in using your registered credentials. Make sure your information is accurate so you can access your account and receive important updates.

What should I do after creating my account?

After registration, log in to your YUÉLÍN account and explore your dashboard. Familiarize yourself with your points balance, Code Redemption section, Rewards page, Missions, Achievements, and other available features. To start collecting points from an eligible YUÉLÍN purchase, navigate to Code Redemption and enter the unique code included inside your purchased product.

Where can I find my YUÉLÍN product redemption code?

Your unique redemption code is included with eligible purchased YUÉLÍN products. Check the spoon inside your product tub to find the code. Eligible products currently include Pastillas Toblerone-Graham Coated and Pastillas Graham Flavor. Keep the code private until you are ready to redeem it, and enter it into your own YUÉLÍN account.

How do I redeem my YUÉLÍN product code?

First, register and log in to your YUÉLÍN account. Open the Code Redemption section, locate the unique code inside your purchased YUÉLÍN product, and carefully enter the complete code into the redemption field. Double-check the characters before submitting. Tap the Redeem button and wait for the system to verify your code. If the code is valid and eligible, the corresponding points will be credited to your account. You can then check your dashboard or points section to view your updated balance.

Do I need to register before redeeming a product code?

Yes. You must have a YUÉLÍN account before you can redeem a product code. The account allows the system to associate the redeemed code and its corresponding points with your personal points balance. If you have not registered yet, create your free account first, log in, and then proceed to the Code Redemption section.

How many points can I receive from a product code?

Each eligible redemption code has an assigned point value. Depending on the code, you may receive different amounts, with a possible maximum of 10,000 points. The actual points awarded are determined by the unique code you receive. Not every code guarantees the maximum amount, so redeem your code and check the confirmation message to see your actual reward.

How much are my YUÉLÍN points worth?

The standard YUÉLÍN points conversion is 100 points = ₱1 in equivalent reward value. For example, 500 points are equivalent to ₱5, 1,000 points to ₱10, 5,000 points to ₱50, and 10,000 points to ₱100. This conversion helps you understand the equivalent value of your accumulated points when browsing eligible rewards. Points are not automatically cash and must be used according to the redemption options and conditions available in the app.

Can I redeem the same product code more than once?

No. Each redemption code has an assigned usage limit. Once a code has been successfully redeemed and its usage limit has been reached, it cannot be redeemed again. This helps protect the rewards system and ensures that each eligible code is used according to its intended conditions. If you receive an error saying that your code has already been used, contact YUÉLÍN support for assistance.

Why is my redemption code not working?

There are several possible reasons: the code may have been entered incorrectly, it may have already been redeemed, it may have reached its usage limit, or it may not be eligible for redemption. Check every character and make sure there are no missing digits, extra spaces, or typing errors. If the code still does not work, take a screenshot of the error message and contact YUÉLÍN support. Include your username and the affected code so the team can investigate. Never post your unused redemption code publicly.

Where can I check my points balance?

Log in to your YUÉLÍN account and open your dashboard or points section. Your available balance should be displayed there. After a successful product code redemption or another qualifying points activity, check your balance again to confirm that the points have been credited. If the expected points do not appear, review your redemption status and contact support if necessary.

How can I earn more YUÉLÍN points?

You can collect points through eligible product code redemptions and other qualifying activities available in the app. Explore the Missions section for tasks, check daily activities, participate in eligible community activities, unlock achievements, and use the referral feature when available. Each activity may have its own requirements, eligibility rules, and point value. Always read the activity description before participating to understand how the reward is earned.

What are Missions and Achievements?

Missions are activities or challenges that you can complete to participate in the YUÉLÍN rewards experience. Each mission has its own description, requirements, and possible point reward. Achievements recognize milestones and progress within the platform. Visit the Missions and Achievements sections to see what is available, check your progress, and learn what you need to accomplish. Rewards are granted only when the applicable requirements have been successfully completed and verified.

How do I redeem my points for rewards?

Log in to your YUÉLÍN account and open the Rewards section. Browse the available rewards, select the item you want, and carefully review its required points, description, availability, and redemption conditions. Make sure your account has enough available points before proceeding. Tap the redemption or claim button, confirm your request if prompted, and follow any additional instructions. Once submitted, your request may require processing or verification. Check your redemption history or the relevant reward status section for updates.

What rewards can I claim using my points?

Available rewards may include mobile load, GCash rewards, Maya rewards, YUÉLÍN products, mystery treats, personalized NFC cards, VIP membership, and other exclusive digital or physical rewards. The actual selection may change depending on availability, promotional periods, and program conditions. Visit the Rewards section inside your account to see the current options, required points, and instructions. A reward appearing in promotional materials does not guarantee that it is currently available for redemption.

Can I convert my points directly into cash?

YUÉLÍN points represent an equivalent reward value, but they are not automatically cash or a guaranteed cash balance. They are primarily intended for redeeming the rewards listed in the app. If GCash, Maya, mobile load, or another cash-equivalent option is available, you can check its required points and redemption conditions in the Rewards section. Availability and eligibility may vary, and a redemption request may require additional processing.

What happens if I do not have enough points for a reward?

You need to collect additional points until your available balance meets the reward's required amount. You can continue redeeming eligible product codes, completing available missions, participating in qualifying activities, and inviting friends through the referral program. Check your points balance regularly and review the Rewards section to see which options are currently within your available balance.

Can I invite friends to join YUÉLÍN?

Yes. If the referral feature is available on your account, you can access your referral link or code through the Refer section. Share your referral information with friends who want to join YUÉLÍN. They should register using your referral link so the system can properly attribute their registration. Any referral rewards are subject to the program's eligibility requirements, successful referral tracking, and qualifying activities.

How does the reseller referral reward work?

Eligible YUÉLÍN resellers who have been properly connected to the referral program may earn 20% referral points based on the qualifying points successfully redeemed by customers attributed to their referral account. For example, if an eligible customer redemption awards 1,000 points, the corresponding 20% reseller referral reward would be 200 points, subject to the program rules and successful tracking. This is a points-based reward, not an automatic cash payment. Resellers must complete the required account registration and referral setup before customer redemptions can be attributed to them.

What is YUÉLÍN VIP Membership?

YUÉLÍN VIP is an optional membership advertised at ₱99 per month. Its listed benefits may include 2× points on eligible activities, one complimentary monthly snack, no transaction fees on eligible transactions, priority customer support, opportunities for point multipliers, and one personalized NFC card. Membership benefits, eligibility, and availability are subject to the current VIP terms. Visit the VIP section of the app and review all details before subscribing.

Can I use YUÉLÍN on my mobile phone?

Yes. YUÉLÍN can be accessed through a compatible mobile browser on your smartphone, as well as through compatible desktop browsers. Open the website, log in to your account, and navigate through the available features. For a smoother experience, use an updated browser and a stable internet connection.

What should I do if my points are missing?

First, check whether your code redemption or qualifying activity was successfully completed. Review any confirmation messages and your redemption or activity history, if available. Refresh your account and check your points balance again. If the points are still missing, contact YUÉLÍN support with your username, registered email, the code or activity involved, the approximate time of the transaction, and a screenshot of any relevant confirmation or error message. Do not attempt repeated redemptions using the same code while the issue is being investigated.

Can I change my registered email or password?

Account information and security settings depend on the options currently available in your YUÉLÍN account. Check your profile or account settings for the relevant update options. If you cannot access the required setting or have lost access to your registered email, contact YUÉLÍN support for guidance. Never send your password or verification credentials in a support message.

Are my points transferable to another account?

Do not assume that points can be transferred between accounts. Points are associated with the account that earned them and are subject to the current YUÉLÍN program rules. Check the app's terms or contact support before attempting to transfer, combine, or share points.

Do my points expire?

Point expiration depends on the applicable YUÉLÍN rewards rules and the terms of the specific points or promotion. Check the relevant information displayed in your account or contact support if an expiration date is not shown. If a reward or promotional activity has a deadline, make sure you review its conditions before participating.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check that you are using the correct registered email or username and password. Make sure your internet connection is stable and that there are no typing errors or accidental spaces. If a password recovery option is available, follow its instructions. If you still cannot access your account, contact YUÉLÍN support with your username and registered email. Never share your password or one-time verification codes with anyone.
